\chapter{Research paper draft attachment}

This appendix attaches a conference-style research paper draft prepared from the thesis work. The draft is formatted separately from the thesis report because conference papers normally use a compact two-column format, while the thesis follows the BITS F423T report structure.

The paper draft is not claimed as a published paper at the time of submission. It is included to satisfy the research-paper draft requirement and to provide a shorter conference-oriented version of the completed work.
